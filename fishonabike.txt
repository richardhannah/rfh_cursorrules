Fish on a Bike

In the sparkling blue waters of Lake Ripple, there lived a clever fish named Finley. Finley was always curious about the world above the waves, especially the shiny, whirling things he saw on the shore.

One sunny morning, Finley spotted a bicycle leaning against a willow tree. With a determined flip of his tail, he leapt from the water, flopping and flapping until he landed right beside it. To his surprise, the bicycle had special water wheels—left behind by a visiting inventor!

With a wiggle and a splash, Finley climbed onto the seat. He pedaled with his fins, and the water wheels spun, sending him gliding across the lake’s surface. Ducks quacked in amazement, and turtles cheered as Finley zipped by, a trail of bubbles in his wake.

From that day on, Finley became the legend of Lake Ripple—the fish on a bike. He explored every cove, raced the dragonflies, and showed everyone that with a little imagination, even a fish could ride a bike.

And so, Finley pedaled joyfully ever after, his heart full of wonder and the thrill of the ride.
